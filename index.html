About Me

I'm a cybersecurity professional with a Master's degree in Cyber Operations from Webster University, where I built a strong foundation in offensive and defensive security practices, network security, threat analysis, and operational security strategy. My graduate studies sharpened my ability to think critically about vulnerabilities, adversarial tactics, and the systems that keep organizations secure in an increasingly complex threat landscape.

Before pursuing my graduate studies, I worked as a Content Specialist at Thomson Reuters, where I gained valuable experience in a fast-paced, professional environment. The role helped me develop strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and the ability to manage information accurately — a solid foundation that supported my transition into a more technical field.

My path into cybersecurity was a deliberate one, driven by genuine curiosity about how systems work, how they can be compromised, and how they can be defended. I'm passionate about continuous learning, staying current with emerging threats and technologies, and applying an analytical, problem-solving mindset to every challenge.
